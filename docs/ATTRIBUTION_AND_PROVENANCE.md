## Data and artifact boundary

The private historical snapshot was obtained through yfinance. The yfinance project states that its library is intended for research/educational use and directs users to Yahoo's terms for rights in downloaded data. This public candidate therefore excludes raw and processed market rows, row-level predictions, and date-level regime labels. It includes only metadata manifests and aggregate research tables.

The four SVG figures were generated by the included project-authored standard-library renderer from four included aggregate CSVs. They contain no external images, scripts, or remote assets.
